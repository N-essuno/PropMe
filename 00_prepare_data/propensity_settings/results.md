## Embedding similarity to the training data

`scripts/embedding_similarity.py` on the seed-42 sample of 10k training documents per corpus (the selection used an independent seed-43 sample). `max_sim`: cosine to the nearest document; `topk_mean`: mean of the 10 nearest; `mean_sim`: cosine to the sample centroid. `generic_<x>_1000` is the Tatoeba prompt set of the corpus (`generic_prompts_1000.jsonl`; the 2k sample without its full matches, 1000 of them at random); `train_<corpus>` are sentences of other training documents (in-distribution reference). Differences: 95% bootstrap CI; AUC: P(random unseen prompt > random Tatoeba prompt).
